Common questions

Why do I have to sign in to my AI provider?

Because NoCap reads your usage from your account. You sign in to the provider’s own website inside the app, exactly as you would in a browser. Your session stays in the system WebView’s cookie store — the app never reads it.

Do my numbers go anywhere?

No. There is no NoCap account and no NoCap server. Everything stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Why are my token figures marked with “≈”?

Because they are estimates. Providers report how much of a window you have used as a percentage, not a token count, so NoCap derives tokens from that. Percentages, resets and streaks are exact; anything marked “≈” is not, and it says so wherever it appears.

Why does it sometimes say a window was not caught?

NoCap can only read your usage while it is open. A window that closes while your phone is in your pocket is recorded as unknown rather than missed — it is never counted against you.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Wipe everything, or delete the app. Both remove your credentials, history and game state from the device. There is no cloud copy to clean up.
