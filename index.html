NoCap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

NoCap collects nothing. There is no NoCap account, no NoCap server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no service on our side that receives it. Everything the app knows lives on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

NoCap shows you how much of your own AI subscriptions you have used, and when they reset. This policy is short because the app does very little.

What is stored, and where

All of it is on your device.

WhatWhere it lives
Connected providers, usage history, streak and game state A database inside the app’s own private storage
API keys and personal access tokens you paste in The iOS Keychain / Android Keystore, never the database
Provider sign-in sessions The system WebView’s own cookie store — the app does not read, copy or transmit these
Display name, handle, theme, ring goal, notification settings The app’s own preferences store

Settings contains a Read the receipts panel that shows these facts from inside the app itself, sourced from the real storage keys rather than retyped.

What leaves your device

Only requests to the AI provider you chose to connect, made with your own credentials, to read your own account. Depending on the provider, that is one of two things:

  • Signing in through a WebView. You sign in to the provider’s own website inside the app, exactly as you would in Safari or Chrome. The usage figure is then read by a request made from inside that signed-in page. Your session cookie stays in the WebView’s cookie store — the app never reads it, stores it, or sends it anywhere.
  • An API key you paste in. Stored only in the Keychain or Keystore, and sent only to the provider it belongs to.

The complete list of hosts NoCap can contact is claude.ai, chatgpt.com, www.perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, openrouter.ai and api.github.com. There are no others, and none of them is ours.

NoCap does not read your conversations, prompts, files, or any content inside your AI accounts. It reads usage counters and reset times.

Backups

Your credentials are stored so that they cannot leave the device even in a backup, and cannot be restored onto a different phone. On iOS the usage database is marked excluded from iCloud backup; on Android automatic backup and device-transfer extraction are both switched off. Your history stays on the phone that recorded it.

Notifications

If you allow them, notifications are composed and scheduled on your device by the app itself. There is no push server. Nothing about your usage is sent anywhere in order to notify you.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Wipe everything. Deletes your credentials, usage history and game state from the device.
  • Delete the app. iOS and Android remove the app’s storage and its Keychain/Keystore entries with it.

There is no account to close and no server-side record to request the deletion of, because neither exists.

Children

NoCap is not directed at children. It is a utility for people who hold paid AI subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because NoCap collects nothing, the most likely future change is a shorter list, not a longer one.

Contact

Questions about this policy go through the support link on NoCap’s App Store listing, which reaches the developer directly.
